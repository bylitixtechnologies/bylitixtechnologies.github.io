How we think

A few things we don't compromise on.

STRUCTURE FIRST

Fix the plumbing first

A report is only as good as the model underneath it. We fix the data model before we touch a chart.

EXPLAIN, DON'T JUST REPORT

What, and why

We reach for causal methods, not just correlation, whenever the question that matters is "why."

BUILT TO BE USED

Remove the friction

Every tool ships with the friction removed — fast to open, obvious to read, nothing to relearn.

FULL ACCOUNTABILITY

One point of ownership

We work as your entire function. Complete ownership from one point of contact.

No dependency for its own sake

If a project can run without a third-party database or framework, it does. Simpler stacks are easier to trust, audit, and hand over.

Beautiful is a requirement

A dashboard nobody wants to open is a failed dashboard, no matter how correct the numbers are. Craft is part of the deliverable.
