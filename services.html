What we do

Six disciplines.
One accountable team.

Most vendors hand you off between a data team, a BI team, and "the AI people." We don't split the work. Build and explain stay with the same people, start to finish.

01

Data Engineering

Pipelines and platforms that move data reliably from source systems into a single, trusted layer — so every report, model and dashboard downstream is working from the same numbers.

  • Ingestion and transformation pipelines on Fabric and Databricks
  • Warehouse and lakehouse design, from scratch or migrated
  • Data quality checks and lineage so issues surface before a report does
  • Documentation the next engineer can actually follow
02

Business Intelligence

Power BI estates built for daily use. Clean semantic models, sensible DAX, and dashboards designed around the decisions people actually need to make.

  • Semantic models and star schemas built for performance at scale
  • Report design, from executive summaries to operational drill-downs
  • Row-level security, governance, and workspace structure
  • Ongoing ownership of an existing Power BI estate
03

Analytics

When "what happened" isn't enough, we reach for the methods that explain why, grounded in how your business actually behaves.

  • Causal inference to separate real effects from coincidence
  • Simulation for decisions under uncertainty
  • Cohort, funnel, and diagnostic analysis
  • Forecasting for what's coming next
04

Machine Learning

Predictive models built to earn their keep in production. Interpretable where it matters, monitored once it ships.

  • Classification and regression with scikit-learn, XGBoost, LightGBM, etc.
  • Model explainability with SHAP for stakeholder trust
  • Graph-based similarity and recommendation engines
  • Retraining, monitoring, and drift checks after launch
05

AI Solutions

Applied LLM systems and agentic tools that call real APIs and do real work — internal copilots, customer-facing agents, and the orchestration layer that keeps them reliable.

  • Agentic applications with tool use and multi-provider fallback
  • Retrieval-augmented systems over your own data
  • Internal copilots for reporting and operations
  • Prompt engineering and evaluation for production reliability
06

Cloud & Consulting

Azure-native architecture and hands-on advisory for teams standing up their first real data platform, or untangling one that's grown faster than its foundations.

  • Cloud architecture and platform strategy on Azure
  • Technology audits and analytics roadmaps
  • Fractional or embedded analytics ownership
  • Team upskilling on Power BI, DAX, and modern data tooling
